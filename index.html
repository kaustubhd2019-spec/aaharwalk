First tool

Daily walking calculator based on BMI

Enter your height, current weight, target weight, and goal duration. The estimate assumes walking creates the full energy gap; in reality, diet and strength work usually matter too.

Second tool

Calories burned from steps, with history

Log steps each day. Data is saved only in this browser using local storage, and you can export CSV or a full JSON backup.
