    How it works & tips
    1. Set an alarm and choose your challenge: squats, push-ups, jumping jacks or dancing, and how many.
    2. At bedtime, tap Start night mode. Keep the phone on charge with RepRise open. The screen goes almost black and stays awake.
    3. Prop the phone up 1.5–2 m away so the camera can see your whole body. Do a practice run first.
    4. When it rings, do the exercise in front of the camera. The alarm only stops when you finish. It gets quieter while you're moving and loud again if you stop.

    Why night mode? Phones don't let websites wake themselves up from sleep, so RepRise has to stay open on screen. If you close it, the alarm can't ring. If you close it while it's ringing, it starts again as soon as you reopen it.

    Volume: turn your media volume up. Everything runs on your phone. No account, no internet needed after the first load, and the camera video never leaves your device.
